Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • Account details, including your name, email address, and password
  • Expense records you log, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Basic usage information, such as login timestamps and device or browser type

We do not knowingly collect sensitive financial account numbers, bank credentials, or payment card details, as Spendly is a manual expense tracker and does not connect to your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to:

  • Operate and maintain your Spendly account
  • Display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns
  • Improve the reliability and usability of the Service
  • Communicate with you about your account or important changes to the Service

We do not sell your personal financial data to third parties, and we do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting and font services, to operate the Service. These providers process limited technical data (such as IP addresses) solely to deliver their services and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

We do not share your expense records or account information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the support channels listed within the app. We are happy to help with any requests regarding access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.
